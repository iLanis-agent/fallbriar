# Fallbriar 1.0 TEST
Native Java/Canvas Android 8+ Stostone rigid-gravity puzzle.

Shade one edge-connected dark stone in each outlined room, with the size shown by its A/B/C/D total. Stones in different rooms cannot share an edge. Now drop each stone straight down without rotating or changing its shape, stopping at the floor or another stone. They must exactly fill the bottom half of the grid. Equal-sized neighboring rooms are allowed. Twelve unique 4x4 room/count boards have twelve distinct solutions.

Saved stone palette and room labels, GG007 icon/menu/site/privacy, unlimited undo/reset. Offline, no ads, analytics, requested permissions or account. Settings and completion marks stay on device. External studio link opens browser.

Independent Java set-based connectivity and reverse-order single-step gravity oracle checks all 65536 shading masks per board against production bitset connectivity and maximal stone descent, proving one solution each. Bounds, toggle, solve, undo and replay pass. Adapter menu, settings, privacy, tile taps, next, levels and persistence pass. Actual drawing-code pixels inspected, not phone screenshots. Phone runtime, performance and browser handoff QA incomplete. Signed TEST only, not final or Play published.

Shared Isleward room-shading and connected-stone backbone is disclosed, but shape-preserving gravity and exact bottom-half packing replace unequal neighboring room sizes. Unlike Twinmarsh isolated dominoes, stones have variable sizes and must fall into a gapless lower half. Known Stostone genre, not a global invention. Full prior local corpus was lost; no ALL-local scan claimed. Rules https://www.nikoli.co.jp/en/puzzles/sto_stone/ and https://jacoblance.wordpress.com/2016/09/19/stostone-rules/ .
